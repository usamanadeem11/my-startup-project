# CUP (Corporate Uplifting Partner)

## 🚀 Mission
CUP is a platform to help people start earning without investment.
We support people who have skills but no money, guidance, or resources.

---

## 🎯 Target Audience
- Students
- Housewives
- Job Seekers
- Madarsa Students
- Beginners who want to earn

---

## 💼 Services Offered

### Business & Skills
- Food Cart Business
- Tiffin Service
- Cloud Kitchen
- Laundry Business
- Automobile Work / Garage

### Health & Wellness
- Body Fitness Program
- Yoga & Meditation
- Remedy for Body Problems

### Personal Growth
- Motivational Programs

### Business Support
- MSME Training Programs
- How to Get a Loan
- How to Start Your Own Business
- Project & Report Guidance

### Jobs & Career
- Job Opportunities in India
- Job Opportunities Abroad

### Education
- Free Coaching Classes (10th & 12th)

### Special
- Madarsa Student Support Program

---

## 💰 Business Model
- Free training provided
- Help in starting business
- Support with tools or capital
- Profit sharing: 50/50

---

## 🌐 Platform Plan
- Website (main platform)
- YouTube channels (business + Islamic content)
- Instagram & Facebook pages

---

## ⚙️ Tech Stack

### Frontend
- HTML
- CSS
- Bootstrap 5

### Backend
- Node.js (Express)

### Database
- MongoDB (planned)

### Payment
- Razorpay (₹150 registration fee)

---

## 📂 Project Structure

client/
- index.html

server/
- server.js

---

## 🎨 Design Style
- Dark theme (navy + black)
- Gold accent color
- Minimal & clean UI
- Premium startup feel
- Mobile responsive

---

## ⚠️ Important Rules
- Save user data ONLY after successful payment
- Keep UI simple and conversion-focused
